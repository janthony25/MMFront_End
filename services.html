Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a car service cost in Auckland?

It depends on your vehicle and what it needs. We always give you a clear price before any work starts — no surprises when you pick the car up. Call us on (+64) 22 426 1587 with your rego and we'll give you an honest quote.

Where are you, and is there parking?

Our workshop is at 38A Huia Road, Otahuhu, Auckland 1062 — a few minutes off the motorway and easy to reach from anywhere in South Auckland. Bring the car to us and we'll take it from there.

Where do your customers travel from?

Our workshop is in Otahuhu, in the heart of South Auckland, and customers drive to us from Mangere, Papatoetoe, Manukau, Otara, Onehunga, Penrose, Mount Wellington, East Tamaki, Papakura and right across the region. We're a short hop off the motorway, so it's an easy run from most of Auckland.

How long will my car be in for?

Most routine jobs — an oil change, a brake service, diagnostics — are done the same day. Bigger repairs and panel work take longer, and we'll tell you up front how long we expect to need before we start.

How do I book an appointment?

The quickest way is our online booking page, where you can pick any free time from Monday to Saturday. You can also call or text us on (+64) 22 426 1587.

Are you open on Sundays?

Not always — Sunday availability varies. We're open Monday to Saturday, 8:30am to 4:00pm. If you need a Sunday, get in touch and we'll let you know if we can fit you in.

What kinds of vehicles do you work on?

We service and repair most makes and models of cars, vans and light commercial vehicles — Japanese, European and Australian. If you're unsure whether we cover yours, give us a call.

Not sure what your car needs?

Give us a call — honest advice costs nothing.
